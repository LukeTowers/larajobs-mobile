Speaker Notes:
But it's not just about bugs. It's about the "Context Switching Tax". Every time we switch from PHP (backend) to JavaScript (frontend) to build a simple feature, we pay a tax. We lose focus. We lose flow. We have to become full-time frontend developers just to add a modal.
