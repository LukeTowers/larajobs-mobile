Speaker Notes:
Hi everyone. My name is Jigar Dhulla.

You know what kills productivity? It's when you have a simple user story—like "Add a Search Bar"—and you find yourself creating **two** Jira tickets.

One for the Backend API. One for the Frontend Component.

And the funny part? They are both assigned to **you**. You are splitting your own brain in half just to ship one feature.

Over the next 15 minutes, I'm going to show you how to reclaim your productivity. We are going to stop splitting our stack and build a highly interactive web app... using just PHP.

I'm going to challenge that idea. I'm going to show you how we can build a highly interactive web app—complete with complex UI interactions like real-time search and infinite scroll—while staying 100% within the Laravel and Livewire ecosystem. No API routes. No complex state management. Just PHP.

Before we dive in to the topic, let me introduce myself.
